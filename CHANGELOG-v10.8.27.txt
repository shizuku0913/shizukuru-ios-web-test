Shizukuru v10.8.27 - Pigment touch-test build

- Activates the approved pigment-strength profile for hands-on testing:
  red 1.00 / yellow 0.90 / blue 1.18 / green 1.04 / cyan 1.08 / magenta 1.10.
- Adds delayed multi-hue saturation dulling with onset 0.52.
- Clean two-colour RYB mixing is deliberately preserved; dulling requires at least three chromatic source paints.
- Dulling depends on hue dispersion and balance, not the number of mixing actions.
- Existing baseline RYB remains available through ShizukuMixingEngine.useRYB().
- Experimental touch-test can be re-enabled with ShizukuMixingEngine.usePigmentTouchTest().
- K/M remains reference-only and does not participate in production mixing.
